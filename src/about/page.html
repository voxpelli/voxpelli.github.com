About me and this site

Hi, my name is Pelle Wessman, but people might know me as VoxPelli on the web. I'm living in Malmö in southern Sweden, just besides the danish capital of Copenhagen.

I'm a web developer who cares deeply about the open web, IndieWeb standards, and building tools that help other developers ship better software. I maintain a bunch of open source packages — ESLint configs, TypeScript tooling, Fastify plugins — and I advocate for owning your content on your own domain.

Previously I've worked at blog reader startup Bloglovin, experienced life as a consultant through Valtech Sweden, taken part of the team behind micro-payment startup Flattr, and grown up as a web developer in the arms of former Malmö web agency Good Old. Before and inbetween all of that there's also been some freelancing, philosophy studies, psychology studies, non-profit work — just to spice things up a bit.

As a web developer I've done tons of things — started out with vanilla PHP at the back-end, went on to WordPress and Drupal and then finally Node.js took over. At the front-end I started out deploying IE6 hasLayout fixes and now it's all CSS custom properties, web components and the rest of the amazing features that the modern web brings. This very site is built with DomStack and async-htm-to-string — types in JSDoc, no build step for templates.

This site

This is my personal blog where I post about coding, the open web, and occasionally life in Sweden. The site is built with DomStack, uses Webmentions for comments, and is hosted on GitHub Pages. It supports RSS feeds, dark mode, and works offline via a service worker.

The design theme is called Sovereign Warmth — warm parchment tones, falu red accents, and a mix of serif and monospace typography. View the source on GitHub.
