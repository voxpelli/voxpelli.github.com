## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The grid-paper ground never renders at <=1440px.** Verified in source: `body` carries the grid background-image; `.layout-wrapper` paints opaque `--color-canvas` over it with `max-width: 1440px`. Ruled lines appear only in the gutters outside the wrapper. This is the north star's first Key Characteristic and the technical half of "warm technical". Fix: move the grid onto `.layout-wrapper`, or make the wrapper transparent. Command: /impeccable layout

- **[P1] The 65ch Rule is violated on the homepage.** Measured: article body = 64.92ch (holds); homepage excerpt paragraphs = 121-137 chars/line at 1440px. The site's own named rule, broken on the page most visitors see first. Fix: constrain `.post-excerpt`. Command: /impeccable layout

- **[P1] Article pages are undated at the point of reading.** The only `<time>` sits at the extreme bottom of the page. The homepage card shows date + read-time above the title, so clicking through REMOVES information. Fix: render the same mono metadata line between h1 and body in article.layout.js. Command: /impeccable layout

- **[P2] The webmention endcap is the least-designed region.** Broken-image glyph for a dead avatar, author "Unknown", self-quote cut mid-word, no heading labelling the responses list. Violates peak-end where it hurts most. Command: /impeccable polish

- **[P2] Excerpt headings pollute the homepage heading outline.** A post excerpt contains a literal `<h2>Background</h2>`, announced to screen readers as a top-level section of the homepage. CSS neutralizes it visually only. Fix: demote structurally in the excerpt pipeline. Command: /impeccable harden
